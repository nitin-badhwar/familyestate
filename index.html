How Balances are Maintained

1. Snapshot Philosophy

In an estate vault, day-to-day fluctuations matter less than knowing where the money is and the order of magnitude. Updating every 1–3 months (or when major deposits happen) is plenty.

2. Quick Balance Updates

Click the icon on any row to immediately adjust the balance and save.

3. Bulk Updating via Excel

Keep a master .xlsx file. When you re-import it, existing account numbers automatically update their balance rather than duplicating.

4. Term & Life Insurance

Keep the "Value" as the full Sum Assured (Coverage Amount) so your family knows the expected payout.
